Why Not Just Use a Free Calculator?

Many insulation manufacturers offer free U-value calculations. They're a reasonable starting point, but they come with limits worth knowing about before you submit anything to Building Control.

Independent, Not Product-Led

A manufacturer's calculator is built to specify that manufacturer's product. Our calculations are independent and product-neutral — we tell you what the build-up actually achieves, not what sells the most insulation.

Condensation Risk Checked

A U-value on its own doesn't tell you whether a build-up is at risk of interstitial condensation. We check this as standard, which free product-selection tools typically don't.

Guidance When It Doesn't Add Up

If a build-up falls short of target, you get an explanation of why and what would close the gap — not just a number and a dead end.

Backed by Regulatory Experience

Prepared by a Chartered Building Surveyor with 45 years in Building Control and Building Standards, so the output is written with Building Control submission in mind from the start.

Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly counts as "an element"?

An element is one distinct type of external wall, roof or floor construction. If your extension has a single wall build-up, a single roof build-up and a single floor build-up, that's three elements. A more complex design with, say, two different wall constructions would be four.

Do you need to visit the property?

No. This is a desktop calculation based on the construction details and drawings you provide, so it can be carried out anywhere in the UK without a site visit.

Can I use this for my Building Control / Building Warrant submission?

Yes. The calculation is prepared with submission in mind, and is backed by RICS chartered status and Building Control experience.

What information do you need from me?

For each element: the layers of the construction in order, their thicknesses, and the materials or products used (including insulation type and thickness). Drawings help but aren't essential if you can describe the build-up clearly.

What if my build-up doesn't meet the target U-value?

We'll tell you clearly, and explain what change would close the gap — for example, additional insulation thickness or a different product. Further advice beyond that is available through our Building Regulations advisory service.

Do you cover Scotland, England, Wales and Northern Ireland?

Yes, UK-wide. The underlying calculation method is the same across the UK; target values differ by nation and by the applicable edition of the regulations, which we account for based on where the property is.

I need a full SAP assessment or EPC, not just a U-value — can you help?
